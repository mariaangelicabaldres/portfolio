Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how I work, what I offer, my tools, and what it's like to work with me as your virtual assistant.

About & Fit

What type of virtual assistant work do you specialize in?

I specialize in executive and operational support — particularly for founders and operators who need someone who can think independently, build systems, and keep operations running without constant direction.

My core areas include:

  • Operations management and workflow design
  • Reporting, dashboards, and tracking systems
  • Email and calendar management
  • Financial tracking and reconciliation
  • Research, summaries, and business documentation
  • Process documentation and SOP creation
What kinds of clients or businesses are you best suited for?

I work best with founders, solo operators, and teams who need reliable, proactive support but don't want to micromanage. I'm a good fit for fast-moving environments where tasks come in loose and varied, and where you need someone who can figure things out independently.

I have hands-on experience with real estate operations, retail business management, and service-based businesses, which makes me adaptable across industries.

What makes you different from a standard virtual assistant?

Most VAs execute tasks. I focus on building systems. I don't just complete what you ask — I look for inefficiencies, create structured processes, and build tools (trackers, dashboards, templates, SOPs) that make things easier going forward.

My background in data and business operations means I bring an analytical lens to everything — I can spot patterns, organise information clearly, and give you better visibility into your own business.

Services & Deliverables

What tasks can you handle on a day-to-day basis?

Day-to-day tasks I can handle include:

  • Monitoring and organising your inbox, drafting replies
  • Managing your calendar and scheduling appointments
  • Taking notes in meetings and organising action items
  • Researching topics and delivering clear summaries
  • Transferring data between systems and maintaining trackers
  • Building and updating Notion pages, templates, and databases
  • Filing documents and keeping shared drives organised
  • Processing job applications, vendor lists, or candidate pipelines
Can you help build systems and processes from scratch?

Yes — this is actually one of my strongest areas. I can design and build tracking systems, financial dashboards, reporting templates, and operational workflows from the ground up. I've built systems that eliminated 8+ hours of manual work per week for clients by automating repetitive reporting and data processes.

Can you handle financial tracking and bookkeeping support?

Yes. I have experience building dynamic P&L statements, reconciling transactions, tracking expenses and revenue across multiple business branches, and producing clean financial summaries for decision-making. I'm not a CPA, but I provide strong operational financial support.

Working Style & Communication

How do you prefer to receive tasks and instructions?

I'm comfortable receiving tasks through messages, voice notes, quick calls, or written briefs — whatever works best for you. I'm used to working with loose or evolving instructions and figuring out the best way to complete things without needing everything spelled out step by step.

How do you make sure nothing falls through the cracks?

I maintain structured task lists and trackers, set clear deadlines for myself, and proactively follow up on open items. If I notice something important being missed or overlooked, I flag it — rather than waiting to be asked.

How do you handle urgent or last-minute requests?

I prioritise quickly and communicate clearly. If something urgent comes in, I'll acknowledge it immediately, give you an honest estimate of when it will be done, and get to work. I understand that in fast-moving environments, priorities shift — and I adapt accordingly.

Are you proactive, or do you wait to be told what to do?

I'm proactive by nature. If I notice something that could be improved, a task that's been missed, or a process that could be made easier, I'll bring it up. I don't wait to be asked for every detail — I take initiative and report back once it's done.

Tools & Technical Skills

What tools do you use most often?

My most-used tools are:

  • Notion — for documentation, databases, and project tracking
  • Google Workspace — Docs, Sheets, Drive, Gmail, Calendar
  • Microsoft Excel — advanced formulas, pivot tables, financial models
  • Slack and Zoom — for communication and meetings
  • Python — for automation, reporting, and data processing
  • ChatGPT and Claude AI — for research and drafting
Can you learn new tools quickly?

Yes — I pick up new tools quickly. If you use a platform I haven't worked with before, I'll get up to speed fast without needing hand-holding. Most productivity and project management tools follow similar logic, so the learning curve is usually short.

How advanced is your spreadsheet work?

Advanced. I'm comfortable with complex formulas (SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS, INDEX/MATCH, VLOOKUP), pivot tables, dynamic dashboards, conditional formatting, and building structured templates that auto-calculate when new data is entered. I've built full financial reporting systems in spreadsheets for multi-branch businesses.

Engagement & Availability

What information do you need to get started?

To get started, I typically need:

  • A brief overview of your business and what you need help with
  • The tools and platforms you currently use
  • Your communication preferences (Slack, email, calls, etc.)
  • Expected workload and any immediate priorities

From there, I'll ask any follow-up questions needed and get going.

Are you available for both short-term and long-term engagements?

Yes. I'm open to both project-based work and ongoing retainer arrangements. Long-term engagements are preferred as they allow me to truly understand your business and become a dependable operational partner.

How is pricing typically structured?

Pricing depends on scope, workload, and engagement type. It can be structured as hourly, fixed-scope per project, or a monthly retainer for ongoing support. Feel free to reach out and we can discuss what makes sense for your situation.
