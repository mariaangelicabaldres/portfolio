The Core Problem

Most real estate decisions are made with incomplete visibility.

A property can look stable while NOI weakens, expenses drift, debt coverage compresses, lease risk builds, or reporting gaps hide what is really happening underneath the monthly summary.

The issue is rarely a lack of data. The issue is that the data is scattered across PM reports, spreadsheets, rent rolls, bank records, CRMs, dashboards, and disconnected workflows.

My work helps operators turn that scattered information into a decision layer — one that makes income, risk, and performance visible enough to act on.

Process

How the work is structured

01
Clarify the decision
Before building anything, define what the work needs to support: buy, hold, sell, refinance, improve operations, reduce expense leakage, monitor performance, or prepare reporting.
02
Reconstruct the numbers
Review the available data: rent rolls, T12s, PM reports, expense categories, debt schedules, dashboards, spreadsheets, or operating workflows.
03
Build the decision layer
Create the model, dashboard, scorecard, reporting system, or operating workflow needed to make the issue visible and the decision supportable.
04
Translate findings into action
The output should show what changed, what matters, where to look next, and what decision the numbers support. Not just a report — a decision tool.
Selected Work

Selected work & modeled case studies

Portfolio Analysis
Real Estate Return System Mapper
Problem
Investors lacked a clear way to compare income, financing, and value performance across multiple assets simultaneously.
What was built
A portfolio diagnostic tool reviewing income, financing, and value systems using NOI, DSCR, expense ratio, financing spread, implied cap rate, and variance flags.
Decision supported
Asset ranking, hold vs. sell evaluation, and capital allocation across a mixed portfolio.
Modeled Case Study
NOI & Expense Visibility Review
Problem
Reported NOI did not reflect operating reality due to misclassified expenses, unaccounted CapEx, and inconsistent vacancy treatment.
What was built
A modeled analysis showing how reported NOI diverges from restated operating NOI when expenses, CapEx, vacancy, and income categories are reviewed at the source level.
Decision supported
More accurate asset valuation, financing assessment, and operating performance benchmarking.
Framework
Residential vs. Commercial Operating Model Comparison
Problem
Investors moving between asset classes were applying residential underwriting assumptions to commercial deals — and vice versa.
What was built
A comparative framework covering underwriting, lease structures, vacancy timelines, tenant risk, financing, and operating risk differences across residential and commercial real estate.
Decision supported
Asset class selection, due diligence scope, and operating assumption calibration across deal types.
Reporting System
Business Intelligence Reporting System
Problem
Business data was scattered across multiple tools with no consistent recurring review structure, making it difficult to surface trends or diagnose performance issues early.
What was built
A reporting system concept converting scattered business data into structured weekly, monthly, and quarterly performance reviews with KPI tracking and variance flagging.
Decision supported
Operational decision-making, performance monitoring, and business health visibility on a recurring basis.
